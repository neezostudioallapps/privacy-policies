# Privacy Policy — Neezo Hematology Atlas

Last updated: 2026-10-21

This policy describes what data Neezo Hematology Atlas ("the program") handles. The program is published by Neezo Studios.

## 1. Data stored on your computer
All your content — the atlas database you build, saved books and papers, clinical case notes, dictionary entries, research ideas and settings — is stored on your own computer. We do not operate a server that receives or stores it.

## 2. Connections the program can make
The program connects to the internet only when you use a feature that needs it. Every connection goes through one gate that can be switched off completely (Settings → Privacy & Network → Offline mode) and that keeps a log you can read (View connection log) with the feature, host, path, outcome and size — never the content. The features are:
- **References:** Crossref and PubMed lookups, searches of scholarly sources in Papers Lab → Web Search, and open copies of papers (PubMed Central, Unpaywall). What is sent is the identifier or the words you typed. If you enter an e-mail address in Settings → Research services, it is sent with those requests as the services ask.
- **Downloads you ask for:** local AI models and tools from their public hosts (for example Hugging Face).
- **Update checks:** the program may ask GitHub for the latest release and content information. In the Microsoft Store build, program updates come from the Store.
- **Cloud AI providers (optional):** if you enter your own API key for a provider (Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic or DeepSeek) and use an AI, speech or embedding feature, the text, image or audio of that request is sent to that provider. Before the first send, a window shows the exact text and asks your permission. The provider's own privacy policy applies. With a local model nothing leaves your computer for these features.

## 3. API keys
Keys are stored encrypted for your Windows account and are not written to plain settings files.

## 4. What we do not do
No analytics, telemetry or usage statistics; no tracking; no advertising; we do not sell or share your data; no online account is required.

## 5. Health-related information
The program is a personal reference and education tool. If you use Clinical Cases, enter de-identified or illustrative data only. You are responsible for any medical-data rules that apply to you.

## 6. Backups and deleting data
The optional backup/export saves a copy of your local data only where you choose; it is never sent to us. You can remove local data with "Restore Default State" in Settings or by uninstalling and deleting the folder `%LocalAppData%\NeezoHemaAtlas`.

## 7. Children
The program is for students, professionals and educators and is not directed at children.

## 8. Changes
A changed policy is published at the same address and included with the next release.
